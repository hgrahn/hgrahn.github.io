Research

Main interests

Computer Architecture, Multicore Systems, GPGPU and Cloud Computing, Parallel programming, AI/Machine Learning, and neuromorphic computing

  • ScEENeC - Scalable, Energy-Efficient Neuromorphic Computing (2026-2030), together with Lund University and Linköping University. Role: PI/Project leader. Funded by ELLIIT - Excellence Center at Linköping - Lund in Information Technology.
